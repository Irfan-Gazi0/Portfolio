BIM-based Immersive Virtual Reality for Element-Level Embodied Carbon Assessment using Machine Learning Optimized Concrete Mix Design

Undergraduate thesis

Supervisor: Dr. Md. Ikramul Hoque

XGBoost predicts concrete-mix embodied carbon at R² 0.95, against 0.54 for Lasso, and the prediction is linked to a virtual reality BIM model at the early design phase, when carbon is hardest to track. Selecting any column or beam will show the carbon amount they could save by switching to greener materials.

Review Paper

Supervisor: Dr. Md. Habibur Rahman Sobuz

A PRISMA-guided review of how deep learning is being flown on construction sites. Safety-Gated LLM Controller for a Hospital Robot

Pythonpy_treesLLM orchestrationDockerpytest

A deterministic six-priority behaviour tree gates gpt-4o-mini's task plans before they reach hospital robot hardware. Emergencies preempt in-flight tasks instantly; medication safety and prompt injection are both refused. 15 unit tests, 8 scenarios.

Token-Budget-Aware Retrieval for Medical Literature

PythonRAGPineconeOpenAIFastAPIReact

A retrieval scorer that packs passages into a fixed 1,800-token budget, trading relevance against redundancy and keyword coverage. Against cosine similarity and MMR over PubMed chunks in Pinecone, it used the fewest prompt tokens (1,734) at the highest ROUGE-L (0.476).

Emergency Response Assistant for Electric Vehicles

LangChainGPT-4oPineconeWhispern8nthree.js

A virtual portal for first responders handling electric vehicle emergencies. It pairs a 360-degree walkthrough of the vehicle with a chatbot that answers with citations from about 578 documents across three Pinecone namespaces.

COVID-19 Misinformation Classifier under Temporal Drift

TensorFlowKerasNLPLSTM

Trained on May articles, tested on July ones, so the distribution shifts. Five experiments across LNN, LSTM and MLP heads over Count and TF-IDF features, scored on minority-class recall instead of accuracy.
