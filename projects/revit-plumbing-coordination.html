Revit-Based Architectural and Plumbing Coordination

Autodesk Revit

Overview

A unified architectural and plumbing model for a commercial building, built in Autodesk Revit. Parametric components keep the hot and cold water supply, the drainage runs and the fixtures consistent with each other and with the architecture. The coordination carries through to the construction drawings, where each supply pipe is annotated with its diameter and bottom-of-pipe elevation.
