Work-Zone Hazard Identification with Vision-Language Reasoning

Irfan Gazi

Abstract

On a highway paving operation, workers on foot move between the paver and the mixer trucks that feed it, and the danger often lies in where a worker stands relative to a machine. Object detection can draw a box around a worker and a box around a truck, but it cannot say that the worker is standing in the truck’s blind spot. This project tests whether vision-language reasoning can close that gap. Drone footage of a highway paving operation is passed to NVIDIA Cosmos Reason, a vision-language model that reasons over the whole clip rather than single frames. The model flags workers inside the operating radius of the paver and the mixer trucks, and recommends controls such as a spotter and a marked exclusion zone. The work is ongoing.
